    What the model is doing

    Hidden state
    The growth stage. A satellite never sees it. The field is in one stage at a time, and the stage usually persists or moves forward.
    Observation
    NDVI, EVI, and NDRE on a clear pass. NDRE often falls while NDVI is still high, which is how flowering separates from grain fill.
    Live monitoring
    The forward filter. The belief on a date uses only passes that had arrived by that date, which is what an operator would have known then.
    Season reconstruction
    The Viterbi path. One stage sequence that best explains the whole series, including passes that came later.
    Learning
    Expectation-maximization updates each stage’s spectral signature from unlabeled passes. It starts from the textbook signature, so a short record cannot invent a backward season.
